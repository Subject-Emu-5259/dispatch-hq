<p><strong>Command the chaos. Save the city.</strong></p>

<p>Dispatch HQ puts you in charge of an entire city's emergency response. Build fire, police, and EMS stations across a real road map, recruit and train your units, then answer the calls as they come in — structure fires, cardiac arrests, high-speed pursuits, hazmat incidents, and more. Every second counts, every unit matters, and the city is counting on you.</p>

<h2>Build your city's lifeline</h2>

<p>Start from a single station and grow into a full command. Place stations where the city needs them, hire the right mix of fire, police, and EMS crews, and train them up so they respond faster and handle tougher incidents. Your layout <em>is</em> your strategy — a badly placed station is the difference between saving a neighborhood and losing it.</p>

<h2>Answer real-time emergencies</h2>

<p>Missions spawn live across the map with real road routing. Dispatch the closest unit, manage response times, and clear the incident before it escalates. Some calls are routine. Others spiral into multi-stage emergencies — a structure fire that spreads, a pursuit that turns into a hostage situation. The city doesn't wait for you to be ready.</p>

<h2>Take command of real cities</h2>

<p>Twelve cities across three continents, each with real street data, precise city-limits boundaries, and unique challenges. City traits shape gameplay — Paris's urban density spawns more structure fires, London's congestion zones slow response, Tokyo's megacity scale means more calls at once. Expand beyond your first city and unlock new maps as you level up.</p>

<h2>Co-op with your alliance</h2>

<p>Team up with other players in an alliance. Share a mission board, dispatch to the same calls, and earn clearance badges together. Pool credits in the alliance treasury, gift credits to help new commanders, build shared stations that cover the whole alliance, and lend units to teammates who need backup. The more you coordinate, the faster your alliance grows.</p>

<h2>City-flavored radio</h2>

<p>Every city has its own dispatch voice. London dispatch says "Acknowledged" and "Make Pumps 4." Paris uses "Reçu" and "FEU." Tokyo dispatches in Japanese-style phrasing. Berlin, Sydney, Toronto — each city's radio chatter, field activities, and mission cards reflect the local emergency services culture.</p>
