# v0.20.1 — Personnel & Station Perks

**Theme:** The command floor gets a pulse — crews tire on long shifts and stations
specialize as they level up. Queued as the two Medium items from
`plans/v0.18.0-release-readiness.md` ("Employee fatigue & morale — long shifts
reduce effectiveness; ties into the patrol shift clock" and "Station
specialization perks — level-ups branch into perks (response radius, jail
capacity, hospital ward)").

## Task 1 — Employee fatigue & morale

- **Model** (`packages/shared/src/types/employee.ts`): `Employee` gains
  `fatigue?: number` (0–100) and `morale?: number` (0–100); `generateEmployee`
  defaults to 0 / 100. Persists automatically inside `station.employees` saves.
- **Simulation** (`apps/web/src/lib/personnel.ts`, pure + unit-tested):
  - `advancePersonnel(stations, gameMinutesDelta)` — assigned crews gain
    fatigue (+0.05/game-min ≈ exhausted after ~33 real min of constant duty),
    idle crews recover (−0.06/game-min), off-duty/training recover twice as
    fast. Morale drains while overworked (fatigue ≥ 70), recovers while resting.
    Values are rounded so the store only rewrites a station when a value
    visibly changes (a few writes/min, not 1/sec).
  - `fatigueFactor` (1.0 → 0.75), `moraleFactor` (1.0 → 0.85),
    `crewEffectiveness`, `stationCrewEffectiveness`, labels.
- **Effects (real, not cosmetic):**
  - **Response**: `dispatchLogic.dispatchUnitToMission` divides the driving ETA
    by crew effectiveness — a burned-out crew rolls out slower.
  - **Clear speed**: App.tsx in-progress progress multiplies by the mean crew
    effectiveness of the assigned units — tired crews take longer on scene.
- **UI:** EmployeesPanel rows show fatigue/morale bars + labels and a
  **Rest / Recall** toggle (off-duty recovers 2×). StationDetailModal roster
  shows compact condition text. Training now also boosts morale (+10).

## Task 2 — Station specialization perks

- **Model** (`packages/shared/src/constants/perks.ts`): `Station` gains
  `perks?: string[]`. Slots at levels **2 / 3 / 5** (max level 5); one perk per
  slot. Three perks per station type:
  - fire: Response Radius (+15% coverage), Suppression Wing (+10% fire/hazmat/
    rescue rewards), Training Hall (−25% training)
  - police: Response Radius, Patrol Presence (+10% police/traffic rewards),
    Dispatch Office (−10% hiring)
  - ems: Response Radius, Community Care (+10% EMS rewards), Paramedic Academy
    (−25% training)
- **Effects:**
  - Radius perk: bumps `coverageRadius` on pick **and** is re-applied at every
    upgrade (`perkAdjustedRadius`) so level-ups don't overwrite the bonus.
  - Reward perks: `stationRewardMultiplier` → new `stationRewardMult` input to
    `computeMissionRewards` (+10% per owning station in the clearing fleet,
    capped +30%); wired in App.tsx mission completion.
  - Economy perks: `hireCost(station)` / `trainCost(employee, station?)` apply
    the discounts (perk-aware at every call site).
- **UI:** StationDetailModal gains a **Specializations** section — a chooser
  when a slot is unlocked, chips for chosen perks, and the Next Upgrade preview
  shows the perk-adjusted radius.
