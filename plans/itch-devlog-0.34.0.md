# v0.34.0 — London joins the roster, the first city across the pond

**Post this on the itch.io dashboard:** agreatgame.itch.io → **Edit game** → **Devlogs** → **Create new post** (paste the markdown below; the dashboard editor renders itch markdown).

---

Dispatch HQ crosses the Atlantic. London is the first non-US city on the ladder, and it gets the full treatment every American city got before it:

- **London radio traffic sounds like London** — dispatch chatter, field activities, and mission cards now name real streets (Oxford St, Fleet St, Brixton Rd, Whitechapel Rd), Thames crossings (Westminster Bridge, Tower Bridge, London Bridge), and landmarks (Big Ben, the Shard, the O2, Wembley Stadium) — with real district names (Westminster, Stratford, Brixton, Greenwich, Harrow) on the dispatch board instead of generic sectors.

- **The precise city-limits boundary** — all 32 boroughs plus the City of London, straight from OpenStreetMap and simplified for the map. Missions, field activities, and station placement stay inside Greater London and never spill into the ring towns just outside it (Watford, St Albans, Slough, Dartford). Fun fact: the City of London sits *inside* the boundary — it's central London where the action happens, so it's part of the dispatch area, not a carved-out neighbor.

- **Regional trauma referral** — when hospitals overflow, London patients divert to the Royal London Hospital, the region's largest major trauma centre.

- **CI keeps growing** — the automated suite gained a London journey (campaign start + station placement against the real boundary), running alongside West Memphis, LA, Chicago, and Houston.
