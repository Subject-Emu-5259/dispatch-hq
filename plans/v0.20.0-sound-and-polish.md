# v0.20.0 — Sound & Polish

> **Goal:** Turn the functional-but-thin synthesized audio (v0.14.0 Task 4) into a
> designed sound kit, and finish the accessibility/UI polish gaps left from the
> v0.18.0 plan. No external audio assets — everything stays pure Web Audio
> synthesis (zero download, zero licensing), but engineered like a real UI sound
> kit instead of bare beeps.

## Task 1 — Sound engine v2 (`apps/web/src/utils/sound.ts`)

Design principles (researched: Material Design sound guidance, sfxengine
frequency-mapping article, procedural UI-kit synthesis write-ups):

1. **Frequency map** — keep families in distinct bands so cues read instantly:
   - `ui` clicks/hovers: 800–1400 Hz, sine, <60 ms
   - `dispatch` (tones, sirens, station-built): 440–1100 Hz, square/saw
   - `alert` (mission, chain, level-up, disaster): 500–1200 Hz, saw + shimmer
   - `ambient`: 196–262 Hz pad + filtered-noise city hum
2. **Tactility** — every click gets a tiny band-passed noise transient (the
   "recorded button" trick) so synthesized clicks feel physical, not digital.
3. **Stereo pan** — clicks/hovers get a small random StereoPanner offset so the
   UI feels spatial instead of mono-center.
4. **Radio realism** — dispatch tones start with a key-up **squelch**
   (band-passed noise burst); `playRadioSquelch` exported and reused.
5. **Real siren wail** — `playSiren` becomes a frequency-swept wail
   (500↔800 Hz saw ramps) instead of two static blips; chases and disasters
   get the genuine rise/fall. Keep `playSirenBlip` for quick roll-outs.
6. **Spam throttle** — per-cue minimum intervals (click 40 ms, hover 120 ms,
   dispatch 1500 ms, alert 800 ms, siren 2500 ms) so mission floods and rapid
   clicks can't stack cues into a wall of noise.
7. **New cues**:
   - `playCash` — quick ascending blips for credits earned
   - `playPursuitAlert` — rapid alternating beeper for pursuit start
   - `playSirenBlip` — short two-tone roll-out blip
8. **Ambient v2** — keep the A3/C4 pad, add a very-low-gain looped
   filtered-noise "city hum" bed beneath it.

## Task 2 — Wire the kit

  cards (`onMouseEnter`).

  render.
- `playCash` → mission-completion credit award in App.tsx (fires with the toast).
- `playPursuitAlert` → when a chase-phase pursuit activates.
- `playSiren` wail automatically improves the existing chase radio.

## Deliberately NOT in v0.20.0

- External audio samples/loops (keeps the zero-asset, zero-license property).
- Background music (musicEnabled exists; a synthesized score is a future task).
- Web Speech dispatcher voices (radio chatter is text-based today).
