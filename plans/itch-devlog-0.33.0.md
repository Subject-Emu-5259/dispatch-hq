It's a city-focused release. Los Angeles radio traffic finally sounds like Los Angeles:

- **LA radio speaks LA** — dispatch chatter, field activities, and mission cards now name real LA streets (Wilshire, Sunset, Figueroa, Ventura), landmarks (Union Station, Dodger Stadium, the Getty), and districts (Downtown, the Westside, the Valley, South LA) — and never the neighboring cities carved out of the city-limits boundary.

- **Also in this release: two more precise cities.** Chicago and Houston joined the roster with their real OSM city-limits boundaries (enclave pockets like Norridge, Bellaire, and the Memorial Villages carved out so calls never spawn in a neighboring jurisdiction), city-accurate street/landmark/district radio traffic, and regional trauma-center referrals (Stroger, Ben Taub). LA, Chicago, and Houston now get the same precise-boundary treatment as West Memphis, Memphis, Nashville, Little Rock, and NYC.

- **The smoke test finishes what it starts** — the automated suite now plays a patrol mission to the end: dispatch, speed up, save + reload mid-mission, and wait for the actual completion toast (+credits/+XP). Two new CI journeys cover a fire-service campaign and city starts with real boundary attachment.

- **Link shares finally show the game, not a logo** — the site ships a real 1200×630 Open Graph/Twitter card with proper metadata.

- **Three new belt-bounty challenge ports** (ER staffing, zone heat scoring, triage phrasing) joined the daily ops queue, validated against the live pipeline.
